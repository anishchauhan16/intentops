How the decision works

Confidence has a threshold.

A TF-IDF and logistic regression pipeline ranks the intents. Below the configured $threshold acceptance threshold, the request needs review.

The optional semantic mode can consult nearby training examples. The classifier probability remains separate from the retrieval vote.
